# PHASE 9 — Procurement & Goods Receiving

Build mobile procurement operations.

## Purchase Orders

Allow authorized users to:

- View PO
- View supplier
- View expected items
- View quantities
- View status

## Goods Receiving

Mobile workflow:

```text
Purchase Order
       ↓
Receive
       ↓
Scan/Create Jewellery Items
       ↓
Verify Weight
       ↓
Quality Check
       ↓
Tag Item
       ↓
Inventory
```

## Quality Check

Display:

- Item
- Weight
- Purity
- Stone details
- Condition
- Certificate

Allow authorized users to approve/reject.

Optimize the receiving workflow for warehouse staff.
