# PHASE 12 — Jewellery Repair Mobile

Build mobile repair operations.

## Repair dashboard

Display:

```text
New
Inspection
Approval Pending
In Progress
Quality Check
Ready
Delivered
```

## Job Card

Show:

- Repair number
- Customer
- Jewellery item
- Images
- Condition
- Problem
- Estimate
- Artisan
- Expected date
- Status

## Camera

Allow authorized staff to capture:

- Before image
- Damage image
- After image

## Workflow

```text
Receive
 ↓
Inspect
 ↓
Estimate
 ↓
Customer Approval
 ↓
Repair
 ↓
Quality Check
 ↓
Ready
 ↓
Deliver
```

Make status updates fast and easy for staff.
